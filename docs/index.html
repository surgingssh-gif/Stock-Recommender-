How It Works

The reading

Each weekday morning the bot pulls the latest business and world headlines from Finnhub, then sends them all to Claude in a single request.

The calls

Claude names a handful of US-listed stocks or ETFs the news could move, says whether it leans bullish or bearish, and explains why in plain English.

The scorecard

Each call is checked against the latest closing price. A bullish call is right so far if the stock is up since it was picked; a bearish call if it's down. No trades are ever placed.
